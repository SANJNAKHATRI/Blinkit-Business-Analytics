## Blinkit Business Analysis - KPI Dashboard in Excel
## Introduction 
I wanted to build an interactive sales dashboard, so I used a Blinkit dataset to analyze key business metrics like total sales, profit, order trends, top products, and regional performance. To make the dashboard engaging, I included light and dark mode options, allowing users to switch themes based on their preference while exploring actionable insights from Blinkit’s business data.

## About Dataset
- 13 columns 
- 8,524 rows 
- The dataset includes details like Item Fat Content, Item Identifier, Item Type,	Outlet Establishment Year,	Outlet Identifier,	Outlet Location Type,	Outlet Size,	Outlet Type,	Item Visibility,	Item Weight,	Sales	Rating, and other <a href="https://github.com/SANJNAKHATRI/Blinkit-Business-Analytics/blob/main/BlinkIT%20Grocery%20Dataset.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Project Objective
The goal of this project is to build an interactive sales dashboard for Blinkit to analyze key business metrics, uncover trends, and provide actionable insights. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor performance across outlets, product categories, and regions, and supports data-driven decisions with visual and interactive elements.

## Key Business Questions
- Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
- How does sales performance vary by outlet type, size, and location?
- What are the top-performing outlets and regions over time?
- How does customer rating correlate with sales and product types?
- What trends can help optimize inventory, marketing, and outlet strategies?
-  Dashboard Interaction <a href="https://github.com/SANJNAKHATRI/Blinkit-Business-Analytics/blob/main/BlinkIT%20Grocery%20Excel%20Report.xlsx">View Dashboard</a>

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales: Overall revenue generated
- Average Sales: Revenue per transaction or outlet
- Number of Items Sold: Total products sold per category/outlet
- Average Customer Rating: Customer satisfaction score
- Sales by Outlet Size, Type, and Location: Insights for operational efficiency
- Sales by Product Category: Identifying high-demand items

 ## Process For Analysis
- Data Collection: Gathered Blinkit sales data across outlets, categories, and periods.
- Data Cleaning: Handled missing values, inconsistencies, and duplicates.
- Data Transformation: Categorized outlets by type, size, and location; calculated metrics like total sales, average sales, and ratings.
- Analysis & Visualization: Explored trends in sales, outlet performance, and product categories. Created interactive charts and graphs.
- Dashboard Design: Built a user-friendly, interactive dashboard with light and dark mode for easy interpretation of metrics.

## Project Insights
- High-performing outlets are mostly Tier 3 and large supermarkets, contributing the highest sales.
- Fruits, snack foods, and household items are the top-selling product categories.
- Tier 3 locations have better revenue despite having fewer items, indicating higher transaction efficiency.
- Customer ratings are consistent across outlets (~4.0), showing overall satisfaction.
- Interactive filters (by outlet type, size, and item category) allow users to explore specific insights quickly.

## Conclusion
This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Blinkit’s business performance, helping identify profitable outlets, top-selling products, and regional trends. It empowers business managers to make data-driven decisions regarding inventory planning, marketing strategies, and outlet operations, with the flexibility of interactive visualization and theme options.
